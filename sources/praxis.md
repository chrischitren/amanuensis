# Praxis

In the course of human events it occasionally becomes necessary to solve problems or build things, and in the course of trying to solve harder problems and build things to loftier specifications, humanity has devised, refined, implemented, forgotten, and disavowed myriad techniques for **organizing action.** Such organized action, at the level of an individual, is what might be called discipline; a willingness to [study](curricula). At the level of direct interaction between individuals, we practice the art of [communication](communication).
