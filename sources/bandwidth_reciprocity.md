# Bandwidth Reciprocity

## Introduction

How much did you have to exercise your agency before you were presented with this content?

It is my experience that interactions feel more meaningful when the ratio of "complexity of response" to "complexity of prompt" is closer to one. By "complexity" I mean something like the bandwidth required to convey or encode the prompt or response, hence the name of the idea, though I must be clear: this is not a quantitative measure.

Algorithmic feeds which release a deluge of relevant, *designed* stimuli at the slightest intimation of interest are, in this respect, terribly imbalanced. The behaviors commonly referred to by terms like **parasocial relationship** or **doomscrolling** are consequences of a newfound ability to reap more than one sows, informationally speaking ("new" is only the magnitude of imbalance; humanity has pursued this ability, and succeeded to some degree, for centuries).

It is important now to distinguish between something *feeling more meaningful* and something *being more compelling.* Were the two conflated, working strictly from this model, it would follow that no student could sit in a classroom without being rapt in the deluge that flows forth from the instructor, no reciprocation necessary! Yet, paradoxically, is it not difficult for most people to attend to the instructor at all? The paradox is none at all if the two notions are kept independent. Indeed, the meaninglessness of poorly-matched bandwidths explains the apathy of a poorly-trained student, assuming people are apathetic toward experiences that feel meaningless.

At the other extreme lies, perhaps, the experience of the instructor. Faced with a mass of human flesh, apparently breathing, the instructor pours intense concentration into the creation and maintenance of an interesting (or at least coherent) narrative, and receives, at best, facial expressions, and at worst, absolutely no acknowledgement of their effort.

It is important now to recognize that this is not the sole wellspring from which "meaning" flows, and to emphatically state that my aim is not to go on a wild goose chase after something as ephemeral and individual as *meaning.* My aim, instead, is to give form to an explanation of the inner vacuousness that arises as a consequence of attending to poorly bandwidth-matched experiences.

## In Search of Meaning: Good Listening

Humans can communicate however they see fit. Despite your free will rendering this technically true, it is generally understood that members of a community are expected to abide by certain unspoken rules. You can explicate these rules if you are [the sort of person inclined to do so](https://www.sas.upenn.edu/~haroldfs/dravling/grice.html). One such rule that I try to follow is thus:

> Listeners shall develop faithful mental models of their interlocutor's ideas.

The fidelity of these models should be limited only by constraints that are (in the present moment) out of the listener's control: the finite human working memory, tiredness, emotional state.

## Understanding; Learning; Feedback Loops; Programming and Compiling

Iterative manipulation of a source that generates outputs will only produce **understanding** if the outputs are withheld from the manipulator for some time, forcing the manipulator to *reason* about what is expected. Too short of a delay, and the designer will be steering on intuition; too long, and the designer will produce malformed sources (w.r.t. the generator's interpretation).
